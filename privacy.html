Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: Adahly has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and we never sell or share your data. Everything needed for prayer and Quran reading works on your phone without an account. Your location never leaves your phone.

Who we are

Adahly (“we”) is an Android app published by Adahly, India. Contact: hello@adahly.com.

Data that stays on your phone

This data is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

  • Location. If you choose “Use my location once”, Adahly reads your approximate location one time to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. If you prefer, you can pick your city from a list instead. Your coordinates and city are stored on your phone and are never uploaded, even if you sign in.
  • Excused days. Days you mark as excused (for example, during menstruation or illness) are stored on your phone. They are never shared with anyone, including a family circle. If you sign in, see below for how they are backed up so that only you can read them.
  • Settings such as your calculation method and alert preferences.

Data stored in your account (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google to use backup, sync or a family circle, we store:

  • Your email address and a Google account identifier, to sign you in.
  • Your prayer marks (prayed on time, late or missed), qada list, Quran reading progress, bookmarks and app settings, so they can be restored and synced between your devices.

Excused days are also backed up, but encrypted on your phone before they leave it, with a key kept in your own Google account. We store only the encrypted data and cannot read it.

Your location is never part of this.

Family circles

If you join a family circle, the other members can see only a daily summary: how many of today’s prayers you have marked as prayed and whether you met your Quran goal. They cannot see your prayer times, individual marks, excused days, qada list or location. You can hide your summary or leave the circle at any time. To send reminders (“nudges”) between members, we store a notification token for your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check which plan you have. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (or your account ID if you sign in) and your purchase records from Google Play.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a technical report: the error, device model, Android version and app version. It contains no personal information. We use it only to fix bugs.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account from the Me screen in the app. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it on our servers.
  • Delete data on your phone by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.
  • Location can be switched off for Adahly in your phone’s settings at any time; the app keeps working with a city you choose.
  • To access, correct or delete your data, or for any privacy question, email hello@adahly.com. We reply within 30 days.

Data retention and security

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Data is encrypted in transit, and each account’s data is protected so only that account can read it.

Children

Adahly is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

This website

adahly.com uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Fonts are served from this site.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
