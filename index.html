What “Adahly” means

A habit, for me.

Adahly joins two Arabic words: ʿādah (عادة), a habit, and lī (لي), for me. It is a reminder that small acts, kept up every day, become part of who you are.

Everything for salah, nothing else

Four things, done properly.

Prayer times and alerts

Accurate times for your city, calculated on your phone with the usual method for your region. Choose a sound, silence or no alert for each prayer.

The Quran, in Arabic

The full Mushaf in Uthmani script, offline. Set a daily goal in pages, ayahs or minutes and continue where you stopped.

Qibla, anywhere

A compass that points to the Kaaba from true north, tells you which way to turn, and works without internet.

Widgets

The next prayer and today’s checklist on your home screen, updated at every prayer time.

Gentle streaks

Streaks that understand your life.

Your lantern stays lit for every day you pray all five. When life gets in the way, Adahly helps you return instead of making you feel guilty.

  • ☾Excused days pause your streak and never break it. They stay private, always.
  • ◆Streak freezes cover the odd missed day so one slip doesn’t undo months.
  • ↺Qada log keeps track of missed prayers so you can make them up.

Questions

Are there translations or tafsir?

No. Adahly shows the Quran in Arabic only, exactly as published by the Tanzil Project, with no translations or interpretations added.

Which calculation methods do you support?

Adahly picks the usual method for your region, such as Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura or Karachi. You can change it, and choose the Hanafi or standard Asr time.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Prayer times, alerts, qibla, the Quran and your streaks all work offline.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Adahly is launching on Android first.
